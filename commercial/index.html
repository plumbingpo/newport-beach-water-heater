Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Newport Beach Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Newport Beach Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Newport Beach and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including failed gas valves, burned-out elements, flame sensor and ignition faults, high-limit lockouts, leaking tanks and fittings, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless units stuck in lockout. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, valving off a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Preventive Care

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Newport Beach Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water hardness, usage patterns, and budget.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses on a regular schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and more consistent water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
